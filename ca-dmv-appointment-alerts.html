FAQ

When does the CA DMV release new appointment slots?
There is no published release schedule; capacity frees up in irregular batches as appointments are rescheduled and cancelled. That unpredictability is precisely why continuous monitoring — not periodic manual checking — is the only reliable coverage.
Why isn't a page-change monitor like UptimeRobot enough?
Because it notifies on any page difference, not on slot availability. A banner edit triggers the alert; a genuinely open Tuesday slot may render identically. You need slot-level parsing between the page and the notification.
Is automated slot monitoring against DMV rules?
Read-only monitoring of public pages at ≤1 req/s with self-service booking is the compliant pattern; aggressive auto-booking bots are what create ToS and BOTS Act issues.

What happens when an alert fires?
